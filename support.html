Guidance for using CalculatorOne+

CalculatorOne+ helps you organize selected photos, videos, audio, and files locally on your iPhone with PIN access.

Getting Started

  • Create your PIN when you first open CalculatorOne+.
  • Import photos, videos, audio, or files from your device, or capture new media with the camera.
  • Use albums and folders to organize content stored inside the app.

Sharing and Inbox

  1. In Photos, Files, Safari, or another supported app, choose a photo, video, audio file, or file.
  2. Open the iOS Share sheet and select CalculatorOne+.
  3. The item is saved to Inbox, where you can preview, organize, move, retry, or delete it.

You can also use From Link to enter a supported HTTP or HTTPS file link, or scan a QR code containing one. The app asks for confirmation before downloading.

Browser Upload, Device Migration, and Backup

Browser Upload: connect your iPhone and computer to the same trusted WiFi network, enable Browser Upload in Settings, and enter the displayed address in a browser. Stop the service when you finish.

Device Migration: open Settings > Backup & Device Migration > Transfer over Wi-Fi. Keep both compatible devices unlocked, open, and on the same trusted WiFi network while transferring.

Encrypted Archive: use Backup & Device Migration to export an encrypted archive, then restore it on another compatible device with the source PIN. Keep your PIN safe; there is no developer recovery service.

Transfer Troubleshooting

  • Confirm every device is connected to the same trusted WiFi network.
  • Allow Local Network access for CalculatorOne+ in iOS Settings if prompted.
  • Some guest, public, VPN, or enterprise networks block device-to-device connections.
  • Keep both devices unlocked and CalculatorOne+ open during Device Migration.

CalculatorOne+ Pro

CalculatorOne+ Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It unlocks Emergency Vault creation, unlimited custom albums and Files folders, encrypted archive export, sending a device migration, and removal of in-app banner ads. It is not a subscription and does not include cloud storage.

To restore a previous purchase, open Settings > CalculatorOne+ Pro and choose Restore Purchases. If a purchase does not appear, confirm you are using the Apple Account that made the purchase and contact Apple Support for billing issues.

Common Questions

Does CalculatorOne+ upload my photos?

No. Content is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to a developer-operated cloud service.

Why does an album not show a preview?

Some album cards use a neutral preview so the library remains easy to scan without displaying media thumbnails on the card.

Can I rename or delete Private Photos?

No. Private Photos is a protected system album and cannot be renamed or deleted.

What happens if I delete the app?

Local content may be permanently removed. CalculatorOne+ is not a cloud backup service, so export important files or maintain an appropriate device backup.
