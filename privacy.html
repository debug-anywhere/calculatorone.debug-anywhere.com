CalculatorOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

CalculatorOne is designed as a private vault for photos and videos. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

Information We Do Not Collect

CalculatorOne does not require an account and does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking data, contact lists, location data, or payment information. We do not operate a cloud service for your vault content.

Photos, Videos, and Vault Content

Photos and videos that you import or capture are stored locally on your device. Vault media is protected on device and is not uploaded to our servers. If you delete the app, the local vault data stored by the app may also be removed by iOS.

App Lock Information

CalculatorOne may store local security settings, such as lock configuration and recovery information, on your device. This information is used only to provide the app lock and recovery experience.

WiFi Transfer

When you enable WiFi Transfer, CalculatorOne starts a temporary local transfer service so devices on the same WiFi network can connect through a browser. The transfer address works only while the feature is active and reachable on your local network. Do not enable WiFi Transfer on networks you do not trust.

Permissions

  • Photo Library access is used to import selected photos and videos into your vault.
  • Camera access is used to capture photos or videos directly into your vault.
  • Microphone access is used when recording videos with audio.
  • Local Network access is used only for WiFi Transfer.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not share vault content with third parties. Apple may process information through the App Store, iCloud backups, crash reporting, or system services according to your device and Apple account settings.

Data Retention and Control

You control the content stored in CalculatorOne. You can delete items inside the app, export content when supported, or remove the app from your device. Content may also be included in device backups depending on your iOS backup settings.

Children

CalculatorOne is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
