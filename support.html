Guidance for using Privela

Privela is a PIN-protected local vault for Photos, Videos, Audio, Files, Notes, and Links. It does not require an account or developer-operated cloud storage.

Getting Started

  • Create your PIN when you first open Privela.
  • Import selected photos and videos, capture media with the camera, or add audio and files from the system pickers.
  • Create notes or save HTTP/HTTPS links from the app or another app's Share sheet.
  • Use albums and Files folders to organize content stored inside the app.

Content Types and Organization

All six content types are first-class entries on Home:

  • Photos and Videos: browse in grid or list mode, preview, sort, and move items in batches.
  • Audio and Files: use list browsing, folders where supported, sorting, moving, and Trash recovery.
  • Notes: create and edit a title and plain-text body; edits replace the encrypted payload without changing the note's identity or join time.
  • Links: save an HTTP/HTTPS URL with a user-facing title, edit it later, open it in the in-app browser, visit privately, copy, share, move, or restore it. The app stores the URL, not an offline copy of the web page.

Photos and Videos support grid/list preferences per Vault and type. Trash normally keeps deleted items for 30 days so you can restore them before permanent deletion.

Sharing and Inbox

  1. In Photos, Files, Safari, or another supported app, choose a photo, video, audio file, file, or URL.
  2. Open the iOS Share sheet or Action extension and select Privela.
  3. The item is encrypted into Inbox, where you can preview, organize, move, retry, or delete it before it reaches a regular album.

Inbox items are retained for 30 days. Items close to expiry show a warning so you can move them in time.

You can also use From Link to enter a supported HTTP or HTTPS file link, or scan a QR code containing one. The app asks for confirmation before downloading and sends a type mismatch to Inbox instead of silently changing the destination.

Browser Access, Device Migration, and Backup

Browser Access: connect your iPhone and computer to the same trusted WiFi network, enable Browser Access in Settings, and enter the displayed address in a browser. You can manage supported local content, including upload, download, rename, and delete actions. Stop the temporary service when you finish.

Device Migration: open Settings > Backup & Device Migration > Transfer over Wi-Fi. Keep both compatible devices unlocked, open, and on the same trusted WiFi network while transferring.

Encrypted Archive: use Backup & Device Migration to export an encrypted archive, then restore it on another compatible device with the source PIN. Keep your PIN safe; there is no developer recovery service.

Transfer Troubleshooting

  • Confirm every device is connected to the same trusted WiFi network.
  • Allow Local Network access for Privela in iOS Settings if prompted.
  • Some guest, public, VPN, or enterprise networks block device-to-device connections.
  • Keep both devices unlocked and Privela open during Device Migration.

Privela Pro

Privela Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It unlocks unlimited custom albums and Files folders, encrypted archive export, sending a device migration, and removal of in-app banner ads. It is not a subscription and does not include cloud storage.

Security and local vaults

Privela provides one app-local encrypted vault. Face ID can unlock the Primary Vault only after you tap the Face ID button on the PIN screen; it is never started automatically. Photos are imported only after an explicit user selection; the app does not hide, delete, or modify assets in the system Photos library.

To restore a previous purchase, open Settings > Privela Pro and choose Restore Purchases. If a purchase does not appear, confirm you are using the Apple Account that made the purchase and contact Apple Support for billing issues.

Common Questions

Does Privela upload my photos?

No. Content is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to a developer-operated cloud service.

Does a saved Link download the whole web page?

No. A Link stores the HTTP/HTTPS address and opens it in the app's browser. Saving or sharing a Link does not archive the page or its web content.

Why does an album not show a preview?

Some album cards use a neutral preview so the library remains easy to scan without displaying media thumbnails on the card.

Can I rename or delete Private Photos?

No. Private Photos is a protected system album and cannot be renamed or deleted.

What happens if I delete the app?

Local content may be permanently removed. Privela is not a cloud backup service, so export important files or maintain an appropriate device backup.
