CalculatorOne Support

Last updated: May 31, 2026

CalculatorOne helps you keep selected photos and videos in a private local vault on your iPhone.

Getting Started

  • Create your app lock when you first open CalculatorOne.
  • Import photos or videos from your library, or capture new items with the camera.
  • Use albums to organize private media stored inside the app.

WiFi Transfer

  • Connect your iPhone and computer to the same WiFi network.
  • Open WiFi Transfer inside CalculatorOne.
  • Enter the displayed address in a browser on the other device.
  • Keep the app open while transferring files, then stop WiFi Transfer when finished.

WiFi Transfer Troubleshooting

  • If the feature is unavailable, confirm your iPhone is connected to WiFi.
  • If the browser cannot connect, make sure both devices are on the same local network.
  • Allow Local Network access for CalculatorOne in iOS Settings if prompted.
  • Some guest, public, VPN, or enterprise networks block device-to-device connections.

Privacy and Backup

Vault content is stored locally in the app. CalculatorOne is not a cloud backup service. Export important files or keep a device backup according to your own backup needs.
