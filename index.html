Six content types

Keep Photos, Videos, Audio, Files, Notes, and Links together as equal content types in the vault.

Inbox from other apps

Send supported media, files, and URLs from the iOS Share sheet or Action extension to a protected Inbox before organizing them.

Search Files, Notes & Links

Find files and folders by name, search note titles and text, or search link titles and saved URLs. Search stays on your device.

Albums, folders, and Trash

Organize content in custom albums and Files folders. Free includes 3 of each per Vault; Trash recovery and existing content remain available.

Independent Primary and Secondary Vaults

Every user can create one independent Secondary Vault with a different PIN. Its encrypted content stays separate from the Primary Vault.

Local transfer tools

Browser Access and device migration use trusted local WiFi; encrypted archives stay under your control and can be restored when you choose.
